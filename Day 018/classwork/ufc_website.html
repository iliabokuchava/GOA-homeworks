The Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) is the world's premier mixed martial arts (MMA)
organization. Founded in 1993, the company revolutionized the combat sports industry and
currently operates under TKO Group Holdings. Starting in 2026, the promotion's exclusive
broadcast home completely transitioned to Paramount+.

  • Meta UFC Rankings: In June 2026, the UFC abolished the traditional journalist voting
    system. Rankings are now calculated using an objective, mathematical Elo-based algorithm
    that weighs opponent strength, recent activity, and performance decay. This system also
    completely eliminated the subjective Pound-for-Pound (P4P) list.
