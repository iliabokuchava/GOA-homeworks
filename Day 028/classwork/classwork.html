BMW M3

The BMW M3 is the high-performance variant of the BMW 3 Series, built by BMW's motorsport division BMW M GmbH and produced in some form since 1986.

The current sixth generation (G80, 2021–present) is a four-door sedan powered across all trims by the twin-turbo S58 3.0-liter inline-six.

The base M3 is the only one with a manual gearbox; the CS Handschalter is a limited, weight-reduced manual RWD variant.
